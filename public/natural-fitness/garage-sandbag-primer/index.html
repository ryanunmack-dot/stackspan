Garage sandbag primer

One soft bag for the garage or driveway — shopping notes and a careful start. Not a program. Not a longevity cure. Not medical advice.

Start here: Fillable soft bag, load it light, nail pickups and carries before you get cute with throws. Progress when form feels boring. We link WOLF Tactical ~100 and ~150 lb capacity via /go — check live Amazon for fill, handles, and price.
Safety: Clear the floor. Shoes with grip. Kids and pets out of the way. Start light — half-filled is fine. Soft bags shift mid-rep, so brace and own the load. Sharp or wrong feeling → stop. New to loaded carries? Get a coach. Not medical advice.

Who this helps

  • Garage / driveway days and you want one awkward load tool
  • Comparing ~100 lb vs ~150 lb capacity soft bags
  • Carries and clean pickups — not a clinic rehab plan

First sessions (stay conservative)

  1. Warm ankles / hips lightly — optional foot-forward warm-up
  2. Fill lighter than you think; practice deadlift-style pickups to a stand
  3. 2–4 short carries (down the driveway and back) with clean posture
  4. Optional: a few controlled clean-to-shoulder or bear-hug holds — stop early
  5. Leave the bag partially filled until sessions feel boring, then add sand

A careful strength start — not a periodized program.

Footwear note

Grip on concrete matters. Wider / flexible trainers are fine for a lot of garage days — shoes hub — but traction and stability beat fashion claims.

Quick answers

100 or 150 lb capacity?

~100 lb for a smaller footprint and lighter max. ~150 lb for one bag with more fill headroom later. Capacity isn’t day-one load — start light either way.

Do I fill it completely on day one?

No. Leave headroom empty until pickups and short carries feel boring. Soft bags shift mid-rep — brace and own the load.

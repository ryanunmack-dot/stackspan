Jack & Elaine LaLanne: the TV fitness pair

By StackSpan Editors

He opened one of the first modern gyms in the US and took exercise to daytime TV. She co-hosted the show, co-wrote the books, and is still doing her floor routine at 100. Two long lives make a good story, not proof of a method: history remembers the people who lived long and forgets the ones who didn't (survivorship bias). Read this as a scrapbook. History and inspiration, not medical advice.

How the facts were checked

Solid Obituaries, newspapers or scholarly sources agree.

Reported Credible secondary sources, but thin or self-reported. Marked "reported" in the text.

Legend Showman stories or later retellings with no primary evidence. None are repeated on this page.

Jack LaLanne (1914–2011)

Jack LaLanne, "Godfather of Fitness"

1914–2011 · reached 96 Solid

  • Solid Opened his Physical Culture Studio in Oakland in 1936, one of the first modern gyms in the US.
  • Solid The Jack LaLanne Show started in San Francisco in 1951 and went national in the late 1950s.
  • Solid At 70, in November 1984, he swam Long Beach Harbor handcuffed and shackled, towing 70 boats for about 1 to 1.5 miles (UPI).
  • Solid Still training and promoting exercise into his 90s.
  • Reported At 93 he told Reuters he trained about two hours a day, roughly 90 minutes of weights and 30 minutes of swimming, and changed the routine every 30 days.
  • Reported As a teenager he cut out sugar and junk food after attending a Paul Bragg lecture.
  • Reported Later he ate raw vegetables, egg whites, fruit and whole grains, and skipped coffee.

“The only way you can hurt your body is if you sit on your rear end and do nothing.”

Jack LaLanne, before the 1984 Long Beach Harbor swim, as reported by UPI.

The blender drink tied to his name is on era training drinks.

Elaine LaLanne (born 1926)

Elaine LaLanne, "First Lady of Fitness"

Born 19 March 1926 · 100 and living (as of spring 2026) Solid

  • Solid Met Jack in 1951, when she booked him to do push-ups on a show she produced. They married in 1959.
  • Solid Gave up smoking and her daily donut after meeting him. She has told TODAY that back then she was eating donuts and smoking, and that Jack told her she should be eating apples and bananas instead.
  • Solid Co-host of The Jack LaLanne Show, co-author, and known as the "First Lady of Fitness."
  • Solid Inducted into the Health & Fitness Association (HFA) Hall of Fame alongside Jack in March 2026.
  • Solid At 100, per TODAY (April 2026): jackknife ab exercises before getting out of bed, leg lifts lying down, and push-ups against a wall or a sink.
  • Solid Food: no named diet. Yogurt with protein powder and berries; salmon or chicken, lots of vegetables, rice or potatoes; cocoa instead of coffee.
  • Solid She has spoken publicly about living with macular degeneration.

Worth noticing

These are patterns in two lives, not causes of anything. Both kept moving regularly well into late life, and both kept food plain. Elaine's routine at 100 starts before she gets out of bed and needs no equipment. Nothing here is a promise about how long anyone lives.

Not medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before changing exercise or diet, especially with a health condition.

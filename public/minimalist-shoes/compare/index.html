Compare wide minimalist / barefoot trainers

A short decision matrix for wide toe-box trainers we actually link — geometry and price tier first, then Amazon /go CTAs. Optional plain brand sites for Xero and Vivobarefoot (not affiliate).

BLUF: Start with toe-box room, zero/low drop, and how easily the sole bends — then choose a price tier. Under ~$40–60 marketplace picks (ASITVO, Airhas, WHITIN) are try-on geometry. Mid-premium known brands (Xero Prio / XT Max, Vivo Motus) cost more and usually make sizing and returns less of a lottery. Shopping and fit language only; not medical advice.

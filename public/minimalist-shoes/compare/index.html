Quick answers

Budget marketplace or known brand first?

Budget (Barezoey / ASITVO / Airhas / WHITIN / UBFEN) is the cheapest way to feel toe-box room. Jump to Xero Prio or X1 Low when sizing and returns matter more than the lowest sticker. Women’s Mid = X1 Mid listing. Short A/B path: starter kit.

Do brand-site buttons earn commission?

No. Xero and Vivobarefoot brand links are plain. Amazon buttons go through our /go affiliate paths. Same retail price either way.

Where’s the fit checklist?

Toe box, drop, flex — that’s on fit & buying. This page is the price lineup.

How to pick from the table

  • Budget try-on: Barezoey, ASITVO, or Airhas first; WHITIN / UBFEN if you want another marketplace band; Joomra when the day is trail-shaped.
  • Known everyday: Xero Prio or X1 Low when brand sizing and returns beat the cheapest option. X1 Mid is the women’s Mid cut — label matches the listing.
  • More cushion / gym: Xero XT Max or Vivo Motus after a first pair feels dialed — not day one.

Still learning the geometry words? Hit fit and buying. First-pair shortlist: beginner or the starter kit. Match the day: by use.
